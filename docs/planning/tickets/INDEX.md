## Status vocabulary

`open` · `in-progress` · `blocked` · `done` · `won't-fix` · `abandoned`

Close a ticket by setting a terminal status and date, moving it from `open/` to `closed/`, removing
its open-queue row, and archiving its pipeline pair. Filenames and ticket numbers never change.
